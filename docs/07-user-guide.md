# 7. User Guide (Task F)

*For PrintXpress customers.* Add screenshots next to each step in your report.

## Getting started
1. **Open PrintXpress.** If you are new, tap **Sign up**.
2. **Create an account:** full name, email, Sri Lankan mobile number (07XXXXXXXX), a password with
   at least 8 characters including letters and numbers, then tick the privacy box and tap **Sign up**.
3. **Log in** next time with your **email or mobile number** and password.

## Home
- **Bell** (top right): your notifications; the orange number shows unread ones.
- **Search bar:** find products quickly.
- **Seasonal offers:** swipe sideways; tap the copy icon to copy a promo code.
- **Categories:** tap one to see its products.
- **Your latest order:** tap to track it.
- **Design help:** Gallery, Print guidelines, FAQs and Support.

## Placing an order
1. Go to **Browse**, search or pick a category, tap a product.
2. Choose a **size** and **material/paper type** (extra cost is shown on the chip).
3. Set the **quantity** with − / + or type it (the minimum and maximum are shown underneath).
4. **Artwork:**
   - *Upload design* → **Choose** → pick a saved design or tap **Upload design** and select a PDF,
     PNG or JPG (max 10 MB) from your phone, or
   - *Enter text* → type what should be printed (name, title, phone…).
5. Choose **Pickup** (free, Colombo branch) or **Delivery** (LKR 350, choose or add an address).
6. Tap the **date** field. Only dates the printer can meet are allowed.
7. Optional: enter a **promo code** and tap **Apply**; add notes for the printer.
8. Check the **price summary** and tap **Place order** → **confirm**. You get a notification and
   the tracking screen opens.

## Tracking and changing orders
- **Orders** tab lists all orders; filter by *Active*, *Completed* or *Cancelled*.
- Open an order to see the progress bar: Pending → Processing → Printing → Ready → Completed.
- While the order is **Pending** or **Processing** you can **Reschedule** (pick a new date) or
  **Cancel**. Once printing starts these buttons disappear.

## Profile
- **Edit profile:** change name, email, phone, or password.
- **My addresses:** add (+), tap to edit, bin icon to delete, choose a default.
- **My saved designs:** upload and delete artwork.
- **Notifications / Offers / Gallery / Guidelines / FAQs / Support.**
- **Log out** at the bottom.

## Staff demo panel (for the demonstration)
Profile → **Staff demo panel** lets you act as PrintXpress staff: tap an order to change its status
(Processing → Printing → Ready → Completed), send an offer to all customers, or reply to a support
question. The customer then sees the alert and the updated tracking screen.

## Getting help
Profile → **Support**: call, email or WhatsApp the team, or type a question. Replies appear under
*My requests* and as a notification.

## Common messages
| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| "The earliest available date is …" | Choose that date or a later one. |
| "Code … needs at least N items" | Increase the quantity or remove the code. |
| "This order can no longer be changed…" | Printing has started — contact Support. |
